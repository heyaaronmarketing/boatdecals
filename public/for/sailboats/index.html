Questions people actually ask

Will these stick to my sailboat?

A sailboat transom is rarely flat. Send a straight-on photo with a tape measure in frame and we will lay the name out to the actual curve.

Do I need registration numbers on it?

If it has a motor on it - including an electric trolling motor in most states - yes. Unpowered hulls usually do not need numbers, but a few states register them anyway. Check with your state agency, then order.

What does it cost?

Registration numbers are $19 a pair including shipping. Names and graphics start at $29 and $22. High-tack vinyl, which your hull may need, is free.
