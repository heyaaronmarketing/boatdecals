Cut vinyl, not printed stickers

Every decal here is cut from coloured vinyl. The shape you see is the only thing that sticks — no clear square, no visible edge, no yellowing background three summers in. That is the difference between a decal and a sticker, and on a boat it is the difference between looking factory and looking stuck-on.

Send artwork, or describe it

Vector files (AI, EPS, SVG, PDF) cut directly. A clean PNG or a photo of the old faded decal works too — we redraw it as vector at no charge, and you approve the proof before it cuts. Multi-colour designs are cut as separate layers and registered together on one sheet so they still apply in one pass.

How it works

  1. Type what it should say. Straight off your registration card for numbers, or whatever you have picked for a name.
  2. Pick height, font and colour. The form locks script fonts out of registration numbers, because the law requires block characters.
  3. We email you a proof. Usually within a few hours during the working day. Nothing is cut yet, and you can still change anything — or cancel for a full refund.
  4. You approve, we cut and ship. Next business day, free, pre-spaced with application tape over the top.

Questions people actually ask

How fast will I get it?

Cut and shipped the next business day, free. Order before 1pm CT and add $12 rush and it goes out the same day. Standard post is 3-6 days in transit.

Can I see it before it is made?

Yes, and you have to. We email a proof and nothing is cut or charged until you reply approving it. If the proof is wrong we redraw it free.

What if I ruin it putting it on?

Tell us and we cut it again free. That covers a torn character, a creased sheet or a set that went on crooked. We would rather send more vinyl than argue.

How long will it last?

The cast film carries a 7-year outdoor warranty on colour and adhesion; standard calendared film runs 5-7 years. Both outlast the 3-4 years typical of the cheapest marketplace sets.
