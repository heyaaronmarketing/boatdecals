What a Florida number looks like

A Florida registration number is built from three parts: the state prefix FL, a block of digits, and a letter suffix. Written out with the spacing the law requires, it looks like FL 1234 AB.

The federal rule, in full. Under 33 CFR 173.27, a registration number must:

  • be painted on or permanently attached to each side of the forward half of the vessel;
  • be in plain vertical block characters of not less than 3 inches in height;
  • contrast with the color of the background and be distinctly visible and legible;
  • have spaces or hyphens between the letter and number groupings equal to the width of a letter other than “I” or a number other than “1” — so TX 1234 AB, not TX1234AB;
  • read from left to right.
33 CFR § 173.27 — Numbers: Display; size; color. Applies in every state. Your state can add to it, but cannot drop below it.

Getting the number in the first place

Register the boat through the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles first. That is where the number and the validation decal come from. We are a vinyl shop, not a registry — we cannot look your number up or issue one, so order once you have the certificate in hand and copy the line straight off it.

The rule most people miss is contrast. There is no required color — but white numbers on a white hull are not “distinctly visible and legible,” and that is a failed inspection. Dark hull, light numbers. Light hull, dark numbers. Our order form flags the combination before it gets cut.

Where people boat in Florida

Florida is the biggest boating state in the country by registrations, and almost none of it is lake boating. The fleet lives on salt water, the Intracoastal and the big inland rivers.

  • Lake Okeechobeeabout 730 square miles, the largest freshwater lake in Florida and the second largest wholly inside the lower 48. Shallow, famous for bass, and a route across the state via the Okeechobee Waterway.
  • The Intracoastal Waterwayruns the length of both coasts. For a huge share of Florida owners the ICW is the home water, not any named lake.
  • The Florida KeysIslamorada, Marathon and Key West - flats skiffs, center consoles and the heaviest sport-fishing concentration in the state.
  • Tampa Bay and Charlotte Harborthe Gulf-coast estuaries, with Boca Grande Pass as the tarpon address every spring.
  • The St. Johns River310 navigable miles running north, one of very few major US rivers that does. Jacksonville to Sanford is cruising water.
  • Biscayne Bay and the Miami Riverthe busiest small-boat water in the south-east, and the one with the tightest enforcement.

The season. Year round. Florida does not have a boating season, it has a hurricane season - June through November - and that is what dictates when boats get hauled.

What has to be registered in Florida

Florida registers: all motorboats; all non-motorized vessels over 16 feet in length.

That is the Coast Guard’s own summary of the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles system. It matters because it decides whether you need a number on the bow at all — the answer for a kayak or a canoe is different in nearly every state, and a boat that does not need registering does not need numbers from us either. If you are not required to register it, we would rather tell you that than sell you a set.

Florida questions

How tall do Florida boat registration numbers have to be?

Not less than 3 inches, in plain vertical block characters. That is the federal minimum under 33 CFR 173.27 and it applies in Florida like everywhere else. Taller is always legal.

What do Florida boat numbers start with?

FL. Your full number runs prefix, digits, then a letter suffix — for example FL 1234 AB. Copy it off your registration card exactly rather than guessing the spacing.

Who issues boat registration in Florida?

You register through the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles. We only make the decals — you get the number from them first, then we cut it.

Do the numbers need a space or a hyphen?

Yes. The rule requires a space or hyphen between the letter and number groups, about as wide as one character. So FL 1234 AB, not FL1234AB. Ours arrive pre-spaced correctly, so you cannot get this wrong.

Where do they go on the boat?

Each side of the forward half of the hull, reading left to right, in a color that contrasts with the hull. The validation decal goes near the number — Florida publishes the exact offset, and it is usually within a few inches.

Do I have to register a kayak or canoe in Florida?

Florida registers all motorboats; all non-motorized vessels over 16 feet in length. If your boat falls outside that, it does not need a registration number on the bow — and it does not need a set from us either. This is the Coast Guard's own summary of the Florida Department of Highway Safety and Motor Vehicles system.

How many registered boats are there in Florida?

1,171,432, which puts Florida first in the country. That is the Coast Guard's 2024 count, from Recreational Boating Statistics, Table 38.

Where are the best places to boat in Florida?

Lake Okeechobee is the one most people name first: about 730 square miles, the largest freshwater lake in Florida and the second largest wholly inside the lower 48. Shallow, famous for bass, and a route across the state via the Okeechobee Waterway. Others worth knowing: The Intracoastal Waterway, The Florida Keys, Tampa Bay and Charlotte Harbor, The St. Johns River.

When is boating season in Florida?
