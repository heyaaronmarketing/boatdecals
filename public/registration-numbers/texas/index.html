What a Texas number looks like

A Texas registration number is built from three parts: the state prefix TX, a block of digits, and a letter suffix. Written out with the spacing the law requires, it looks like TX 1234 AB.

Texas has roughly 922,000 registered vessels, which makes it one of the larger boating states in the country — and means a lot of numbers get replaced every spring when the old set chalks out.

The federal rule, in full. Under 33 CFR 173.27, a registration number must:

  • be painted on or permanently attached to each side of the forward half of the vessel;
  • be in plain vertical block characters of not less than 3 inches in height;
  • contrast with the colour of the background and be distinctly visible and legible;
  • have spaces or hyphens between the letter and number groupings equal to the width of a letter other than “I” or a number other than “1” — so TX 1234 AB, not TX1234AB;
  • read from left to right.
33 CFR § 173.27 — Numbers: Display; size; colour. Applies in every state. Your state can add to it, but cannot drop below it.

Getting the number in the first place

Register the boat through the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department first. That is where the number and the validation decal come from. We are a vinyl shop, not a registry — we cannot look your number up or issue one, so order once you have the certificate in hand and copy the line straight off it.

The rule most people miss is contrast. There is no required colour — but white numbers on a white hull are not “distinctly visible and legible,” and that is a failed inspection. Dark hull, light numbers. Light hull, dark numbers. Our order form flags the combination before it gets cut.

Ordering for a Texas boat

Type the number as it appears on your card, pick a colour that contrasts with your hull, and leave the height at 3 inches unless you want it bolder. You will get a proof by email showing the exact layout before anything is cut, and a full refund if you do not like what you see.

Texas questions

How tall do Texas boat registration numbers have to be?

Not less than 3 inches, in plain vertical block characters. That is the federal minimum under 33 CFR 173.27 and it applies in Texas like everywhere else. Taller is always legal.

What do Texas boat numbers start with?

TX. Your full number runs prefix, digits, then a letter suffix — for example TX 1234 AB. Copy it off your registration card exactly rather than guessing the spacing.

Who issues boat registration in Texas?

You register through the Texas Parks and Wildlife Department. We only make the decals — you get the number from them first, then we cut it.

Do the numbers need a space or a hyphen?

Yes. The rule requires a space or hyphen between the letter and number groups, about as wide as one character. So TX 1234 AB, not TX1234AB. Ours arrive pre-spaced correctly, so you cannot get this wrong.

Where do they go on the boat?

Each side of the forward half of the hull, reading left to right, in a colour that contrasts with the hull. The validation decal goes near the number — Texas publishes the exact offset, and it is usually within a few inches.
