# Photographs

Real photographs, for the listing slots that want them (Google Business
Profile in particular ranks profiles with real photos above logo-only ones).

**Nothing in here is generative AI, and nothing in here may be.** These are
pictures of real people and real work; an invented one presented as either is
fabricated proof. See ../CAPTIONS.md for the same rule applied to the
illustrative imagery.

## owner-headshot.png

The shop owner, from a phone photo taken 2024-11-29 (IMG_1515.HEIC).
Processed with crop, white balance, exposure and a light unsharp mask - no
generative editing, so the face is exactly what the camera recorded.

- Rotated upright; the original was shot sideways.
- Cropped to head and shoulders. This is also what removed the dog sitting on
  his lap: it is out of frame, not painted out.
- White balance pulled 65% of the way to neutral off the grey t-shirt, which
  read (198,182,152) under tungsten light against pine walls. Going the whole
  way looked like a different room.
- Brightness +18%, contrast +12%, saturation -8%, unsharp 2.4px/85%.

The source is soft - low light, slight motion blur - so there is no fine
detail to recover and upscaling would only add pixels. At the sizes these
slots actually display (170-400px) it reads fine.

Sized copies are generated into brand/photo/ by media/brand.py.
